# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 30, 2026

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how **Sudoku Idle: Solver Lab** (“the App”) handles information when you use it. The App is developed and published by **Baborama Studios**.

The App is an idle puzzle game: you solve Sudoku puzzles, earn in-game money, and build a solver that plays for you.

We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how data is handled.

## 2. Data Collection and Sharing Summary

Baborama Studios does not collect your data, does not operate a server for the App, does not require an account, and does not sell your data.

Your game progress and settings are stored locally on your device only.

The App shows optional rewarded ads through **Google AdMob**. When ads are requested or shown, Google's advertising software in the App collects certain device data, as described in section 6. Ads only play when you choose to watch one.

## 3. Information Stored on Your Device

The App stores the following locally on your device:

* **Game Progress:** Puzzles, placed numbers and notes, upgrades, research, in-game money, statistics, and the time you were last active (used to calculate rewards while you were away)
* **Settings:** Sound settings and similar preferences
* **Ad Rewards:** When your optional 2× money boost ends

This data is used only to run the game. It is not sent to Baborama Studios.

## 4. Save Files You Export

The App lets you export your save to a file and import it again. An exported file contains only your game progress. It is written to a location you choose, and the App does not upload it anywhere. If you share that file, you decide who receives it.

## 5. How Your Data Is Used

Your game data is used to:

* Save and restore your game
* Calculate progress and rewards, including while you are away
* Show your statistics

We do not use your game data for analytics, profiling, or marketing.

## 6. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App offers two optional rewarded ads: doubling your welcome-back reward, and a 2× money boost for a limited time. The App never shows ads you did not ask for.

To request and show ads, the App uses the **Google Mobile Ads SDK** provided by Google. Google may collect and process, among other things:

* **Device and advertising identifiers,** such as the Android advertising ID
* **Approximate location,** derived from your IP address
* **App interactions,** such as when an ad is requested, shown, or completed
* **Diagnostics and performance information,** such as crash logs

Google uses this data to serve and measure ads, to prevent fraud and abuse, and, where you have allowed it, to personalize ads. This data is collected by Google, not by Baborama Studios. Google's handling of it is described in:

* [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
* [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

## 7. Your Ad Choices

* **European Economic Area, United Kingdom, and Switzerland:** The App asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalized ads are used. In these regions, an **Ad privacy choices** button appears in the App under **More → Options**, where you can change your choice at any time. Outside regions where this consent applies, the button is not shown; use your advertising ID settings below instead.
* **Advertising ID:** On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on your device).
* **Not watching ads:** All ads in the App are optional. You can play the whole game without watching any.

## 8. Permissions

The App uses the following permissions:

* **Internet and Network State:** To load ads
* **Advertising ID and Android ad services:** Used by Google's advertising software as described in section 6

The App does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, or precise location. Importing or exporting a save uses your device's standard file picker, and only the file you choose is read or written.

## 9. Data Retention and Deletion

Your game data stays on your device until you delete it:

* **Clear App Data:** On supported devices, open your device settings, select the App, and clear its storage or data. This deletes your game progress and settings.
* **Uninstall the App:** Uninstalling removes the App and its locally stored data, subject to your device operating system's normal behavior.
* **Exported Save Files:** Delete them with your device's file manager.

Because Baborama Studios does not receive your game data, we cannot access, recover, or delete it on your behalf. For data collected by Google for advertising, see Google's policies linked in section 6.

## 10. Account Creation

The App does not require or support user accounts. There is no account data for Baborama Studios to delete.

## 11. Data Sharing

Baborama Studios does not sell, trade, rent, or share your data. Advertising data is collected directly by Google as described in section 6.

## 12. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and Baborama Studios does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the App and you have concerns, contact us at the address below.

## 13. Security

Your game data is stored locally on your device. You can protect it by securing your device with a passcode, fingerprint, face unlock, or other security options.

The updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised “Last updated” date.
